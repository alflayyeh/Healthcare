Methods (summary from WebFetch):
- 2023 IHP Survey, adults 18+, 10 countries: AUS, CAN, FRA, DEU, NLD, NZL, SWE, CHE, UK, US. Samples 750-4,820; RR 6-49%.
- Mode: most countries probability landline+mobile phone; UK added online probability panel; Sweden & Switzerland population registry samples, phone+online; US address-based sampling + probability panel + prepaid cell, mobile phone & online.
- Fieldwork March-August 2023 (SSRS).
- Income groups: respondents rated pretax household income vs national average ("much above" .. "much below"). Somewhat/much below grouped as "lower or average income" (per page); somewhat/much above = "higher income". National averages from statistical offices.
- Sweden EXCLUDED: "data could not be provided on average annual household income in Sweden."
